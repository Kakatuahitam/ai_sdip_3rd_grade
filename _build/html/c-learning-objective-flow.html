Learning Goals Analysis (Analisis Capaian Pembelajaran)¶

Before going to know Artificial Intelligence Learning Goals Analysis, let us understand first how Artificial Intelligence characterize itself. Artificial Intelligence subject has been introduced for children with Erasmus+ project named “Artificial Intelligence Education for Children” that is supported by European Commission for Erasmus+ Program.

Its objective are to create education system that is expected to fulfill the coding related job vacancies and to prepare Artificial Intelligence readiness in three aspects; (1) Artificial Intelligence is being put at the service of European Citizens so it should be more encouraged to be researched, (2) Artificial Intelligence will be key factor to economic development, and (3) pursuing competitive Artificial Intelligence for countries or region. So, the project is intended to develop educational materials aimed to children for the teachers who teaches Artificial Intelligence fundamentals based on visual coding experiences with various games and activities.

Plugged and Non Plugged activities (3rd for Non Plugged?, 4th, 5th) Keep in touch in playable and interactive session for each meeting Doing in groups is such more convenient than managing per person Non plugged activities has significant reliance on physical activities and demonstration and needs some creative properties to understand abstract concept

Start from introducing, using, then analyzing

As it is group learning, perhaps no one will be left behind

3rd Grade

  • Non Plugged 70%
  • Abstraction / Imagination 30%, Concrete 70%

4th Grade

  • Non Plugged 50%
  • Abstraction 50%

5th Grade

  • Non Plugged 30%
  • Abstraction 70%

Students will get:

  1. Artificial Intelligence understanding
  2. Utilize Artificial Intelligence with AI Ethics boundaries

Some methods that can be done

  1. Simulating
  2. Visual Coding
  3. Prototyping
  4. Role Playing

Finally, the elements of Artificial Intelligence subject are:

  1. Computational Thinking Students are encouraged to paving their way step by step into true goals by utilizing any resources they have carefully and logically
  1. Digital Awareness and Artificial Intelligence Ethics Students are making their self to be digitally obey and know what and how to do in instant and vast digital world to guide and protect them self and to harness Artificial Intelligence wisely to prevent any risk impacted to students
  1. Human Intelligence and Artificial Intelligence Students are identifying and understanding Human Intelligence superiority that can create an Artificial Intelligence to give aid in working some tasks that Human Intelligence can do but require more complex and faster working time exceeding Human Intelligence can do

Learning goals on Artificial Intelligence is based on Graduation Profile of SD Islam Pembangunan that one of them is Lulusan yang menguasai Teknologi Digital that has its child profile those are:

  1. Menguasai dasar-dasar algoritma
  2. Menguasai dasar-dasar ilmu komputer
  3. Menguasai dasar-dsaar sains
  4. Menguasai dasar-dasar program
  5. Menguasai aplikasi teknologi sederhana
  6. Terbiasa memanfaatkan teknologi digital

And hopefully Artificial Intelligence covers most of the Graduation Profile on two phases of learning (Phase B and C).

Artificial Intelligence Learning Goals¶

Based on reference from Artificial Intelligence book from Turkey and kind of kids nature in B and C Phase, kids are being so active in their motoric so it gives option to enhance everyday learning in moving bodies while percepting the subject we learn on the class.

To keep the learning outline always in a good way of easy and self learning, this Artificial Intelligence subject has these main Element:

  1. Human and Computer
  2. Robotics and Artificial Intelligence
  3. Artificial Intelligence Ethic

Furthermore, this Artificial Intelligence for Phase B has these Learning Goals:

Artificial Intelligence Learning Goals for Phase B¶

  1. To know what is Computer and its other form
  2. To know how computer works
  3. To know how computer interact with human
  4. To know what is Artificial Intelligence really is
  5. ...

Artificial Intelligence Learning Goals for Phase C¶

  1. To know and create computer program for helping daily activities
  2. To understand how Artificial Intelligence is developed and utilized in programs
  3. To understand how to build robotic tools and utilize it for daily activities
  4. To understand how to integrate Artificial Intelligence on Robotic Tools implementation
