a. Learning Outcome Analysis

SD Islam Pembangunan

Learning Outcome Analysis (Analisis Capaian Pembelajaran) is the main goal of that SD Islam Pembangunan aims to be reached for the children to realize one of Graduate Student Profile that is Lulusan yang Cakap Teknologi Digital.

The points of the profile are:

  • Menguasai dasar-dasar algoritma
  • Menguasai dasar-dasar ilmu komputer
  • Menguasai dasar-dasar sains*
  • Menguasai dasar-dasar program
  • Menguasai aplikasi teknologi sederhana
  • Terbiasa memanfaatkan teknologi digital*
Note

*Some points may also be realized by collaborating with subjects value or some specific value like Science subject

Those points stated in Graduate Student Profile is the base of Artificial Intelligence Learning Outcome. Strengthen by statement that states this era is needing Artificial Intelligence agent that will fill the gap in the knowledge base of Artificial Intelligence in the public and professional space. So that learning Artificial Intelligence is enforced since early school stage in this case elementary school.

Students will be taught Artificial Intelligence subject as local content that SD Islam Pembangunan pursue to educate and enlight elementary students to know, learn, adapt, utilize, and even create Artificial Intelligence products since B and C Phase as a part of implementing Kurikulum Merdeka.

To create more enjoy and simple learning for B (3rd and 4th Grade) and C (5th and 6th Grade) Phase, the learning session will be conducted in two main form. Those are plugged and Unplugged sessions that will be changes transiently from 70% of unplugged session and 30% plugged session starts from 3rd Grade to the 70% of plugged session and 30% unplugged session. It is designed to facilitate students to fulfill their body movement needs as the children is designed to keep playing with each others actively in form of physical activities. And it is also helps to regulate screen time management for students that is limited to prevent overtime in screen activities. Finally, the academic output of Learning will be improved as their physical activity has been enhanced cognitive function such as attention, memory, and problem solving. Hiroshi (2024).

Learning Journey¶

From 3rd to 5th grade, we will conduct AI Journey system to clearly paving the path of learning AI since clearly

Goals:

  • Understanding and Creating AI Agents that is fully working by adding, enhancing or modifying its abilities

  • “Membuat Makhluk”

3rd Grade¶

  • Fundamental knowledge of what is AI Agent
  • Fundamental knowledge of coding
  • Playing and pretending with and to be AI Agent

4rd Grade¶

  • Moderate knowledge of coding
  • Creating a software with AI Agents
5rd Grade¶
  • Advanced knowledge of coding
  • Adding physical features to AI Agents

Methods for learning¶

  • Simulation (using the AI or other products)
  • Live Coding
  • Storytelling
  • In Groups
  • Individuals

Module Pattern¶

  • Using story to make students immerge in Learning
  • Who is the character?
  • Chapter based learning with story included

Whole Syllabus¶

  • Session 01 - Welcome to Artificial Intelligence -- Quick, Draw!

  • Session 02 - Getting started with Scratch -- Exploring scratch and joining classroom and sound effect intro

  • Session 03 - Moving your Scratch Character I -- Move and Turn

  • Session 04 - Moving your Scratch Character II -- Go to and Glide

  • Session 05 - Entering Maze I -- Create movement path with previous features, move and turn, go and glide

  • Session 06 - Entering Maze II -- Displaying Coordinate and keymapping 101

  • Session 07 - Entering Maze III -- Sensing by touching other object, object collision

  • Session 08 - Entering Maze IV -- Add mob to avoid
